For everyone

Advice that fits you.

SKIN

Sunscreen is the anti-ageing step

A broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning does more for long-term skin than any serum. Two finger-lengths for face and neck.

All
SHAVING

Fewer bumps, smoother shave

Shave after a warm shower, use a sharp blade, go with the grain first, and rinse with cool water. Replace blades often.

Men
MAKEUP

Skin first, colour second

Hydrated skin holds makeup better. Moisturise, let it settle for two minutes, then apply SPF before any base.

Women
HAIR

Wash for your scalp, not your length

Shampoo cleans the scalp; conditioner is for the lengths. Oily scalp may need more frequent washing; dry scalp less.

All
BEARD

Treat the skin under it

Wash the beard with a gentle cleanser, use a light beard oil, and comb daily to stop itch and flakes.

Men
BROWS

Frame, don’t fight

Follow your natural shape. Tidy only stray hairs outside it, and brush brows up before any shaping.

Women
MOVE

Twenty minutes beats zero

Consistency matters more than intensity. A short daily session builds the habit that big plans never do.

What we believe

Three principles.

i

Simple wins

A few good habits repeated for months beat a perfect routine abandoned in a week.

ii

Evidence first

No miracle claims. We prefer well-supported basics over trends and over-promising products.

iii

Made for everyone

Any gender, any skin, any budget, any starting point. Wellness is not one look.
