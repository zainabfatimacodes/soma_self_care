About Soma

Wellness, edited.

Soma (Greek for “body”) is a small journal with a simple belief: caring for yourself should be calm, clear and free of hype.

We write for everyone — men, women and anyone in between — and for every budget. Our advice leans on widely accepted guidance from dermatology, sports science and public-health bodies, in plain language, with no miracle claims.

Soma shares general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. When something worries you, see a qualified professional.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Soma only for women?

No. Every chapter is for everyone, and the Grooming page lets you switch between men’s, women’s and shared advice.

Where is my data saved?

Quiz results, habits and checklists are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I trust the calculators?

They’re estimates based on standard formulas. They’re starting points for conversation, not diagnoses.

Are products recommended?

We focus on ingredients and habits rather than brands, so the advice works at any price.

I have a skin or health condition. What should I do?

See a doctor or dermatologist before changing treatment. Use Soma for general lifestyle ideas only.
