    Cool-down flow

    Stretch it out.

    Six gentle holds after a workout, or any evening. Breathe slowly; never bounce.

    01

    Child’s pose

    Hips back to heels, arms long. Releases back and shoulders.

    45SEC
    02

    Cat-cow

    Round and arch the spine with your breath.

    10BREATHS
    03

    Low lunge

    Back knee down, hips forward. Opens the hip flexors.

    30SEC / SIDE
    04

    Seated forward fold

    Hinge from the hips, soft knees. For the hamstrings.

    45SEC
    05

    Figure-four

    Ankle over opposite knee, lean in. Eases glutes and hips.

    30SEC / SIDE
    06

    Legs up the wall

    Lie back, legs vertical. Calms the body before sleep.

    Four rules

    The boring things that work.

    01

    Form first

    Slow, controlled reps beat fast, sloppy ones. Quality builds strength and protects joints.

    02

    Progress slowly

    Add a rep, a round or a few seconds each week. Small steps compound.

    03

    Recover

    Muscles grow during rest and sleep. Two rest days a week is a feature, not laziness.

    04

    Fuel & hydrate

    Protein with meals, plenty of water, and carbohydrates around training. See Health.
