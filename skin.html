Skin,
understood.

Your skin is your largest organ and it asks for very little: clean, hydrated, protected. Here is how to do that for your type, in plain language.

The foundation

Four steps. That’s the whole secret.

Everything else is optional. Get these four right, every day, before you buy anything else.

1

Cleanse

Remove dirt, oil and sunscreen with a gentle, non-stripping cleanser. Twice a day is enough; skin should never feel tight afterwards.

2

Treat

Optional targeted serum for one concern — acne, dullness, ageing. Add one product at a time, a few weeks apart.

3

Moisturise

Locks in water and supports the skin barrier. Even oily skin needs it — choose a lighter gel formula.

4

Protect

Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, and reapply about every two hours in strong sun.

Hair care

Healthy hair starts at the scalp.

A few small rules cover most hair types — for any length, any gender.

Oily scalp, limp hair

Wash more often with a clarifying or gentle shampoo, focus on the roots, and put conditioner on the lengths only. Don’t over-brush.

Dry, frizzy hair

Wash less often with a sulfate-free shampoo, use a rich conditioner and a leave-in, and let hair air-dry when you can. Lower the heat on styling tools and use a protectant.

Curly and coily hair

Moisture is the priority. Detangle with conditioner in, finger-comb or use a wide-tooth comb, and sleep on silk or satin or in a loose bonnet.

Shedding or thinning

Some daily shedding is normal. If you notice sudden or patchy loss, or a widening part, speak to a doctor or dermatologist — causes include stress, iron, thyroid, and genetics, and early advice helps.

Dandruff and itch

Try a shampoo with zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole or selenium sulfide, leaving it on for a few minutes. If it persists, see a professional.

Makeup basics

Less, placed well.

  1. PrepMoisturiser, then SPF. Wait a couple of minutes.
  2. Even it outSkin tint or concealer only where needed. Blend with fingers or a damp sponge.
  3. Add lifeA touch of cream blush or lip tint in a shade close to your natural flush.
  4. DefineBrush brows up, curl lashes, one coat of mascara. Done.
  5. RemoveAlways cleanse before bed, even when tired.
FIND YOUR UNDERTONE

Check your veins

Greenish veins usually suggest a warm undertone, bluish a cool one, and a mix means neutral. Gold jewellery flatters warm; silver, cool.

TOOLS

Clean brushes weekly

Dirty brushes and sponges trap oil and bacteria, which can cause breakouts. Wash with mild soap and air-dry.

FOR EVERYONE

Concealer is not just for women

Anyone can use a little concealer or tinted balm for tired eyes or blemishes. It’s grooming, not a statement.

Myth or fact

Myth?

“Oily skin doesn’t need moisturiser.”

Tap to flip
Myth

Skin that lacks water can still make oil. A light gel moisturiser helps balance it.

Myth?

“Higher SPF means you can stay out all day.”

Tap to flip
Myth

Any SPF wears off. Reapply about every two hours and after swimming or sweating.

Myth?

“Pores can open and close.”

Tap to flip
Myth

Pores aren’t muscles. Warm water loosens oil but they don’t open or close; cleansing keeps them clear.

Fact?

“You need sunscreen on cloudy days.”

Tap to flip
Fact

Much of UV passes through cloud and even windows. Daily SPF is the habit that counts.
