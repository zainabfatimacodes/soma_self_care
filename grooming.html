For him

The comfortable shave

Soften

Shave after a warm shower, or hold a warm wet towel on the face for a minute.

Prep

Apply a real shaving cream or gel and let it sit for 30 seconds. Skip the pressurised-foam shortcuts if you’re prone to irritation.

Go with the grain

First pass in the direction of hair growth with light pressure. Let the blade do the work.

Re-lather if needed

For a closer finish, lather again and go across or lightly against the grain only where your skin allows it.

Cool rinse

Rinse with cool water, pat dry, don’t rub.

Calm & protect

Alcohol-free balm or lotion. Replace blades every 5–7 shaves, or sooner if they pull.

For her

Smooth skin, no irritation

Exfoliate first

A gentle scrub or cloth the day before helps prevent ingrown hairs.

Wet & lather

Shave after a few minutes in warm water. Always use shaving cream or a conditioner, never dry.

Short, light strokes

With the direction of growth, with a clean, sharp razor. Go slowly around knees and ankles.

Rinse & pat

Cool rinse, pat dry, no friction.

Moisturise

A fragrance-free lotion or oil within a few minutes. Skip deodorant right after shaving underarms if it stings.

Other options

Waxing, sugaring, epilating and laser each have trade-offs; patch-test and consult a professional.

Beards

Grow it, or keep it clean.

0–1 MM · STUBBLE

Designer stubble

Use a trimmer with a guard, define the neckline and cheek line, and moisturise the skin underneath.

2–6 MM · SHORT

Short beard

Wash with a gentle cleanser, comb daily, use a light balm and keep a crisp neckline about two fingers above the Adam’s apple.

10 MM+ · FULL

Full beard

Brush, oil nightly, and trim ends every few weeks. The first month itches — don’t quit early.

Fragrance

Scent, with restraint.

Where to apply

On moisturised pulse points — wrists, neck, behind the ears. Moisturised skin holds scent longer. Don’t rub your wrists together.

How much

One to three sprays is plenty. If you can smell it strongly on yourself all day, others probably can too.

Match the moment

Fresh citrus and aquatic notes for heat and the day; warm woods, amber and vanilla for evening and winter.

Test before you buy

Spray on skin and wait an hour. Scent changes as it dries down. Check the label if you have sensitive skin.

Hygiene essentials

The quiet basics.

MOUTH

Fresh & healthy

Brush twice, floss daily, clean your tongue. Replace the brush every three months.

BODY

Clean & dry

Wash daily, dry well between toes and in folds. Use deodorant; antiperspirant if you sweat heavily.

HANDS & FEET

Care for the ends

Trim nails straight across for toes. Moisturise feet at night and wear breathable socks.

CLOTHES

Fresh & fitting

Clean, ironed and well-fitting clothes outdo expensive ones. Fresh underwear and socks daily.
